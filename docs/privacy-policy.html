grails.de Muscle Grail Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 3, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Muscle Grail handles information in the iPhone app, the Apple Watch app with its complication, the iPhone widget, and the Mac app.

Overview

Muscle Grail is designed to work without the developer ever receiving your data. It does not require account creation, does not run a server of its own, and does not collect personal data for analytics, marketing, or account management.

Information You Enter

The app stores what you enter to track your training: your plans and their exercises, the sets, reps and weights you log, your workouts and their history, and for any exercise the name, description, video link and photo you give it.

The app does not ask for email addresses, phone numbers, or account credentials, and it does not create user accounts.

The app does not use cookies, analytics, advertising identifiers, or behavior tracking technologies.

On Your Devices

Everything you enter is stored on your devices first, so the app works without internet. Your settings, such as the app's language and the sets and reps a new exercise starts with, are kept in the app's settings on the device.

Deleting the app removes what it stored on that device.

iCloud Sync

Your plans, workouts, history and exercises sync between your devices through your own private iCloud database, under your Apple ID. Your daily step and distance goals, and each day's steps and walking distance (so the Mac can show them), sync through your iCloud key-value storage.

This data is stored by Apple under Apple's Privacy Policy. The developer has no access to your private iCloud database. You can delete the app's data in iCloud from your device's iCloud settings.

Apple Health

With your permission, the app reads your steps, walking and running distance, workouts, heart rate and active energy from Apple Health, to show your activity, your gym days and your statistics, and to sync in the background when Health records new steps.

With your permission, the app saves your finished strength workouts to Apple Health. On the Apple Watch, a workout records your heart rate and active energy.

Health data is never used for advertising or marketing, and never shared with the developer or any third party. You can change these permissions at any time in the Health app or your device's settings.

iPhone and Apple Watch

During a workout, your iPhone and Apple Watch pass the sets you log directly to each other over Bluetooth, so they work without internet. Nothing in this passes through a server.

Training Partner

If you share plans with a training partner, they are shared through iCloud sharing between your Apple ID and your partner's. Your partner receives the shared plans: their names, pictures and exercises, the sets and reps of exercises added to them, and the names of your own exercises in them. Your partner sees your name as iCloud shows it on the share.

Your workouts, weights, history and Health data are not shared. Either of you can stop sharing at any time.

Exercise Illustrations and Videos

The app loads exercise illustrations from the public RepDB exercise dataset on GitHub (raw.githubusercontent.com). Like any web server, GitHub receives your device's IP address as part of the request. Nothing else is sent.

A video link opens in your browser or the YouTube app: your own link, or a YouTube search for the exercise's name. That site's own privacy policy then applies.

Plan Assistant on the Mac

When you describe exercises to add to a plan, Apple's on-device model reads your description on your Mac. It is not sent anywhere by the app. Dictation, if you use it, is provided by Apple under Apple's terms.

Claude on the Mac

The Mac app can let Claude, Anthropic's AI assistant, create and extend your plans. This is off until you switch it on in Settings. It then runs a server on your Mac that only apps on the same Mac can reach.

If you connect Claude to it, Claude can list your plans, search the exercise catalog, create plans and add exercises. What Claude reads this way is processed by Anthropic under your own agreement with Anthropic and Anthropic's Privacy Policy. The developer receives none of it.

Import, Export and Backups on the Mac

GymBook files you import, history you export, and the backups the Mac writes stay in the files and folders you choose. The app does not send them anywhere.

Third-Party Services

The app does not use third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

It uses Apple's iCloud and Apple Health for your data, GitHub to load exercise illustrations, and Anthropic's Claude only if you switch it on and connect it.

If you have chosen in your device's settings to share analytics with app developers, Apple may provide the developer with anonymous crash reports.

Children’s Privacy

Because the developer does not collect or store personal data, the app does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any updates will be published on this page.

Contact

Questions about this Privacy Policy may be directed to the app developer through the contact method provided on the app’s App Store product page.

App Store Privacy Summary

For App Store privacy labeling purposes, this app is intended to operate without collecting data and without tracking users across apps or websites. Your training data and the Health data the app uses stay on your devices, in your own private iCloud, and with the training partner you choose to share plans with. None of it is accessible to the developer.
