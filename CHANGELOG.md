# Changelog

## 1.0 (current)

### Plans
- Build plans on the Mac from a searchable catalog of over 800 exercises with photos, or add your own, each with target sets, reps and weight.
- Make your own exercises, and give any exercise a description, a video link and a photo. Renaming an exercise keeps its history.

### Training
- Train on the iPhone or the Apple Watch, fully offline. Each set prefills from the set just done, else from last time, else from the plan.
- Log a set with one tap; swipe a logged set to delete it.
- Finished workouts go to Health, and the watch records heart rate and calories.
- Train on the Mac too. Its workouts sync like the others but don't go to Health.

### Sync
- Plans, workouts and history sync through iCloud in the background, without opening the app.
- Training works in every app version. Once a newer version has changed your plans, editing them asks for an update.

### Watch face
- A complication shows today's steps and walking distance.

### Languages
- English and German.
