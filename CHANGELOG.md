# Changelog

## 1.0 (current)

### Plans
- Build plans on the Mac from a searchable catalog of over 800 exercises with photos, or add your own, each with target sets, reps and weight.
- Make your own exercises, and give any exercise a description, a video link and a photo. Renaming an exercise keeps its history.
- On the Mac, add exercises to a plan by describing them, typed or dictated, in English or German: "3x10 bench press at 60 kg, then squats". Needs Apple Intelligence.
- On the Mac, let Claude create and extend plans through MCP: switch it on in Settings, where the commands for Claude Code and Claude Desktop are.
- Exercise search finds every word anywhere and ignores plurals and hyphens: "dumbbell curls" finds Dumbbell Bicep Curl.

### Training
- Weight steppers only offer weights the gym has, by the exercise's equipment: dumbbells 20 → 22 kg, barbells in 2.5 kg steps, kettlebells in their sizes, machines and cables in 5 kg steps.
- Plans take turns: the plan after the one you did last comes first as "Next up", and every plan shows when you last did it.
- Train on the iPhone or the Apple Watch, fully offline. Each set prefills from the set just done, else from last time, else from the plan.
- Log a set with one tap, or tick off a whole exercise like "3 × 10 at 20 kg" with one tap; swipe a logged set to delete it.
- Exercise photos show while training, on the watch too, once they're on the device. They download while there is network; in a gym without internet the workout runs as usual and leaves missing photos out.
- Finished workouts go to Health, and the watch records heart rate and calories.
- Train on the Mac too. Its workouts sync like the others but don't go to Health.

### History
- Daily goals for steps and walking distance on the iPhone, with today's progress and a chart of every day like GitHub's. Goals sync through iCloud.
- An activity chart like GitHub's shows the last year day by day: gym workouts, and on the iPhone also walks and hikes from Health.
- Statistics per week, month, quarter or year: workouts per period as a chart, and the current period's workouts, sets and volume.

### Sync
- Plans, workouts and history sync through iCloud in the background, without opening the app.
- Training works in every app version. Once a newer version has changed your plans, editing them asks for an update.

### Watch face
- A complication shows today's steps and walking distance.

### Languages
- English and German.
